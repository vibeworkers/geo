# Evidence Boundary

## Labels

- `captured audit finding`: present in the raw audit pack.
- `recommendation`: an action proposed by the audit pack or derived from it.
- `methodology assumption`: a scoring or interpretation rule from the packaged
  methodology.
- `requires live validation`: needs current evidence outside this package.

## Measurement Limits

The packaged scores are readiness and heuristic comparison signals. They are
not proof of:

- current AI crawler access
- current ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, or Grok answers
- observed AI citation
- referral traffic
- conversion lift
- server-log crawler visits

Before making those claims, collect fresh evidence such as current robots
fetches, HTTP status tests, schema extraction, AI answer captures, analytics,
or server/WAF logs.

## Source Priority For Future Checks

For future live verification, prefer English-language official or primary
sources for crawler behavior, robots rules, structured data, and platform
policy. Use Korean sources as local context or market clues unless the claim is
specifically about Korean institutions, Korean law, Korean market behavior, or
Korean-language public discourse.
